Find Your Marbella

More than a destination — a way of life. Sun, sea, gastronomy, golf and a vibrant international community, all year round.

One destination. Different ways of living.

Marbella East

The emerging luxury side of Marbella.

From Cabopino through Elviria, Santa Clara and towards Los Monteros, Marbella East combines some of the area's most beautiful beaches and golf courses with a more relaxed, distinctly European and Spanish way of living.

With established residential communities, international schools, golf and easy access to the coast, the area has long offered an exceptional quality of life. Today, however, Marbella East is entering a new chapter.

Significant investment and major new developments are bringing a new level of luxury, hospitality and lifestyle to the eastern side of Marbella. Santa Clara in particular represents this evolution — combining established communities with a new generation of high-end residential and hospitality concepts.

With developments such as Dorado and Four Seasons adding to the area's growing luxury offering, Marbella East is no longer simply an upcoming area. It is becoming one of the most exciting and desirable parts of the Marbella coastline.

Adding to the appeal is Kimpton Los Monteros, one of Marbella's established luxury coastal hotels, with its iconic La Cabane Beach Club by Dolce & Gabbana.

With internationally recognised brands such as Four Seasons, Waldorf Astoria and Dolce & Gabbana, Marbella East is emerging as one of the most prestigious stretches of coastline on the Costa del Sol.

For buyers looking for beaches, golf, space, lifestyle and long-term potential — with the opportunity to be part of Marbella's next chapter — Marbella East offers a compelling proposition.

The Golden Mile & Puerto Banús

The Golden Mile is synonymous with luxury. From beachfront living and elegant residences to some of Marbella's most established addresses, it remains one of the most recognisable luxury destinations in Europe.

Further west, Puerto Banús brings a more social and visible side of Marbella — yachts, restaurants, designer boutiques and a lively international scene.

These are mature, well established locations with enduring international appeal.

Nueva Andalucía

Golf, energy and international living.

Nueva Andalucía combines established residential living with the energy of one of Marbella's most international communities.

Surrounded by golf courses and close to restaurants, cafés and everyday amenities, it offers a dynamic lifestyle where there is always something happening.

It is an area where golf, family life, socialising and international living come together naturally.

Its established infrastructure and consistent international demand make Nueva Andalucía a steady and proven area for lifestyle.

San Pedro de Alcántara

Connected and lived-in.

San Pedro offers a different side of Marbella.

More local in character, it combines traditional Andalusian roots with modern international living. Its town centre, boulevard, beaches, restaurants and growing residential areas create a genuine sense of community.

For buyers looking for a more authentic day-to-day lifestyle while remaining close to Marbella, San Pedro offers an attractive balance between local character and international convenience.

As an established area with continued improvement and demand, it also offers steady long-term value.

Benahavís

Space, nature and privacy.

Just inland from Marbella, Benahavís offers a completely different experience.

Mountain landscapes, open surroundings, prestigious golf communities and larger private residences create a sense of space, privacy and tranquillity.

It is a natural choice for buyers who value views, nature and a slower pace of life, while still wanting Marbella and the coast within easy reach.

With good supply, an established reputation for quality properties, Benahavís offers an enduring investment profile.

Estepona

Spanish character

Further west, Estepona offers a more distinctly Spanish interpretation of life on the Costa del Sol.

Its old town, traditional streets, beaches and promenade create a strong sense of place and a lifestyle that feels genuinely connected to southern Spain.

At the same time, significant residential, infrastructure and hospitality developments are bringing increasing international attention to the area.

This combination of authentic Spanish character and continued development makes Estepona one of the areas to watch on the Costa del Sol.

For buyers looking for a relaxed coastal lifestyle, genuine Spanish character and the potential of an evolving market, Estepona offers a different proposition from established Marbella — while remaining within easy reach of everything the region has to offer.
